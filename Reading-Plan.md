# Cornerstone: Person (5710) — Reading Plan

**Fall 2026 · ~495 pages · ~33 hours of reading total**

The syllabus budgets reading at 15 pp/hr (this is *slow, devotional* reading — you're meant to practice the disciplines, not skim). Two credits = ~80 hours total: 22 in class, ~33 reading, ~18 writing, ~4 final, ~4 buffer.

**The golden rule from the syllabus:** *read early enough to practice each assigned discipline more than once before class.* Several weeks have a practice (fasting, prayer, confession, Lectio Divina) that only works if you start days ahead. The plan below front-loads those.

## ⚠️ The three "don't get caught" moments

1. **Dec 14's Nouwen (90 pp).** By far the biggest single reading. The syllabus tells you at Dec 7 to *start it then*. Better: read ~15 pp/day across the last two weeks and it's painless.
2. **Practices with lead time.** The 24-hr fast (Oct 5), the week-long prayer practice you begin *around Oct 26* for Nov 2, and Lectio Divina (Nov 16) all require starting before class. Put them on your calendar now.
3. **Late Reading Responses are not accepted — ever.** And the Rhythm of Life loses a full letter grade per day late. Treat Dec 16 (Rhythm) and Dec 18 (Final) as hard walls.

### How to choose your 8 (a few honest observations)
- **Weeks with a practice (2, 3, 5, 7, 10) are richest** — you'll have lived experience to write from, which makes for a stronger, easier response. Leaning into these is a good default.
- **Nov 23 (Gratitude)** is a gift: no class, over the break, and self-contained — an easy one to bank.
- **Dec 14 offers two responses in one week.** Handy if you're short, but it's also the heaviest reading week, so don't rely on both as your finale.
- **Skip candidates** if you need to drop four: pick from the non-practice, heavier-reading weeks (e.g., 6, 9, 11) — but note you *still must read* every week whether or not you write, since unannounced quizzes can happen.
- **Aim for even spacing.** Don't leave 6 of your 8 for December. A comfortable rhythm: write for ~2–3 of the four September/October weeks, ~3 in November, ~2 in December.

### Two alternative formats (per week, your choice)
- A **4–5 minute video** instead of writing, or
- A **curated image set** (60–90 min of searching for high-quality images capturing the reading's concepts).
